# Title
Granted: Your AI Grants Team for Small Nonprofits | Live Product Demo

# Description
A small nonprofit should not have to spend a week on the wrong grant.

Granted helps small teams find relevant federal funding, check whether they can apply, and build a proposal grounded in their own programs and outcomes.

Try the app: https://grantedai.web.app
Source code: https://github.com/shi1720/granted

In this demo, we load Brightpath, a fictional sample nonprofit, search live Grants.gov opportunities, compare a relevant grant with one that excludes nonprofits, and use the Strategist, Writer, Reviewer, and Reviser to create an editable proposal. We then review missing evidence and track the opportunity.

Built by Shivam Gupta for the AI Builders Hackathon 2026.

Technology: Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, OpenAI Responses API, Zod, Grants.gov, Firebase Hosting, Google Cloud Run, and Secret Manager.

The screen captures show the working application. Waiting periods are shortened for clarity. Narration uses an AI-generated voice. Brightpath's organizational data is fictional. AI recommendations and scores are planning aids, not official funder evaluations or funding guarantees. Review all facts and the full funding notice before submitting an application.
